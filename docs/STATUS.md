## Current workflow versus experiments

The [generic workflow](../references/generic-sprite-sheet-workflow.md) documents the standard route. At this snapshot, its automatic left/right walk route uses video; other action/orientation pairs use sheet generation. Read the actual reference before assuming that every request uses video.

Native workflows are available only for their documented profiles and explicit construction scope. They are not a universal offline replacement for provider video.

The endpoint-cycle and regional-video modules are experimental work on difficult motion or local defects. Their existence and passing technical tests do not establish that the pending golem animation is solved. They should not be presented as the normal beginner entry point.

## Known limitations

- Providers may change anatomy, textures, proportions, direction, or attachment of equipment.
- An image-repetition score cannot establish correct gait or weight transfer.
- Horizontal centering does not fix incorrect anatomy, pose, or scale.
- Alpha cleanup can leave edge artifacts or damage subject pixels; the output requires inspection.
- Fresh provider contracts and pricing may become unverifiable, correctly blocking submission.
- No universal cost, first-attempt success, or automatic artistic pass is promised.
- Legacy workflows contain project-specific assumptions, including a fixed historical reviewer identifier.
- Some legacy tests require private source artwork. Unrestricted test discovery is not the supported clean-install check.
- No public visual gallery is included until artwork redistribution rights are documented.
- A clean application-level installation and engine integration remain separate checks.
